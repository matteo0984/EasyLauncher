Step-by-step guide: get your IGDB API keys

EasyLauncher — 100% Portable & Open Source Retro Frontend

What is IGDB and why do you need a Twitch account?

IGDB (Internet Game Database) is one of the most complete game databases in the world (covers, descriptions, genres, release years, developers, ratings). It is operated by Twitch / Amazon.

Unlike other apps that route requests through a central server, EasyLauncher runs 100% locally on your PC with no intermediate servers. To query IGDB directly, you need a free Client ID and Client Secret from your Twitch Developer account.

Key lifetime: Client ID and Client Secret never expire (unless you delete or regenerate them). EasyLauncher refreshes the temporary access token automatically.
STEP 1

Sign in to Twitch and enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Twitch requires Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to register a developer application. Without 2FA you will see the error: "user must have two factor auth enabled to perform this action".

  • If you do not have a Twitch account, create a free one at https://www.twitch.tv/.
  • Open your account security settings and enable 2FA (Authenticator app or SMS):
Open Twitch Security (Enable 2FA)
STEP 2

Open the Twitch Developer Console

Once 2FA is active, open the Twitch Developer Console and click Register Your Application:

Twitch Developer Console Register New Application
STEP 3

Fill in the «Register Your Application» form

  • Name: pick a unique name (3–100 chars, e.g. EasyLauncher or EasyLauncher123).
  • OAuth Redirect URLs: enter exactly https://localhost.
  • Category: select Application Integration.
  • Client Type: select Confidential (required to get a Client Secret).
  • Click the purple Create button at the bottom.
Watch out for the two most common mistakes:
1) Use https://localhost (with HTTPS).
2) Do not click Add if you only need one URL; remove any empty second field.
STEP 4

Copy «Client ID» and generate «Client Secret»

  • Go back to Your Applications and click Manage.
  • Copy the Client ID at the bottom.
  • Click New Secret right below it and copy the Client Secret immediately.
STEP 5

Enter the keys in EasyLauncher

  • Open EasyLauncher → Tools → Download Metadata → Metadata Settings.
  • In the IGDB (Twitch Developer) section, paste Client ID and Client Secret.
  • Click Save Settings: credentials are stored locally in data/config.json.

Official IGDB documentation: https://api-docs.igdb.com/#account-creation
